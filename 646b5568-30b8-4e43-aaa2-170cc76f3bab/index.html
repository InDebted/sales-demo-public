Frame

We've spent the last decade building InDebted into a market leader operating across nine countries. The path to success has always been the same — partner with exceptional clients, deliver best-in-class performance and consumer experience, and grow our business by growing with our clients.


United Kingdom is a strategic market for us, and Carmoola is exactly the kind of digital-native lender we partner with best. The job today is to walk you through the operating model, our products, and take you "behind the scenes" on what makes InDebted different — calibrated for UK regulation (FCA), local consumer expectations, and the shape of your used-car loan book.


We're doing this so that, by the end of the session, we have a shared view of what a partnership could look like and a clear next step that we both own — to the benefit of your customers, your stakeholders, and the regulators we all answer to.
