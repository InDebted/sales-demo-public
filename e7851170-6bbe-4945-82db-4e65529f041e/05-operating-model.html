01 Account upload / data exchange
01.1
File format
ADCB format differed from ours. Initially received multiple files across products rather than a single unified file.
Repeated upload failures and manual rework on each batch.
✓ Resolved Agreed on a single file in ADCB format. ADCB drops files on our SFTP in the agreed layout.
01.2
Column-naming errors
'Card number' used where 'account number' is expected. Due amounts and credit limits appearing in the wrong fields.
Blocks automation. Auto-upload disabled — every file requires manual cleaning before it loads. Delays account activation.
✗ Open ADCB to tighten file validation before sending. Re-enables auto-upload into Collect.
01.3
Bad data after load
Wrong account numbers / balances loaded. Once in Collect these fields can't be corrected — workaround is a manual transaction upload, which distorts account history.
Manual effort per account. Misleading records that affect treatment logic and reporting.
✗ Open Files must be accurate before upload. ADCB to confirm data integrity as part of the handoff process.
01.4
Missing data fields
Still not receiving last payment date, date of birth (credit-card customers), and a linked-liabilities indicator (Yes/No).
Treatment sequencing weaker without last payment date. Tracing harder without DOB. No visibility on linked liabilities — the bank requires all linked products to settle together, including those not assigned to us.
✗ Open ADCB to add these fields to the upload file. Linked-liabilities flag is the most critical of the three.
01.5
Balance changes without txn
When balances change (bank fees, interest), an updated balance is sent without a supporting transaction record.
Outstanding balance mismatches between Collect and the bank's records. We currently re-upload accounts without reflecting the change.
✗ Open Each balance change accompanied by a transaction record so the account balance is accurate and we collect against it correctly.
02 Reporting
02.1
Payment report format
ADCB required password protection and decimal formatting on the payment report.
Compliance and formatting requirement.
✓ Resolved Done. Report delivered by 10am T+1 UAE time.
02.2
Real-time payment view
ADCB want to see payments as they come in, not on a T+1 basis.
Visibility gap between when a customer pays and when ADCB sees it.
■ In progress ADCB has Stripe dashboard access for real-time payment visibility. Otherwise the T+1 report is the fastest written report available.
02.3
Duplicate payments
Same payment reported more than once across successive payment files.
Distorts commission calculations and reconciliation. Auto-upload of payment files is disabled — every file needs manual review and dedupe before loading.
✗ Open ADCB to resolve duplication at source. Re-enables auto-upload of payment files.
02.4
Payout report (T+5)
Payout report delivered at T+5 business days (7 calendar days). Driven by Stripe settlement timing in the UAE, not InDebted.
Delays ADCB's ability to reconcile fund movements against core banking.
■ In progress InDebted continues pushing Stripe, but timing is largely out of our hands. In the interim, both sides to explore solutions. See Payments below for the underlying constraint.
02.5
Activity report (Genesys)
ADCB needed visibility on collection activity (calls, SMS, emails). Genesys call data has a ~24h lag from our data lake.
Activity report cannot yet reflect same-day calls.
■ In progress Activity reports now delivered daily. Working on reducing the Genesys lag — target is next-day automated reporting, max 2-day lag.
03 Payments
03.1
Stripe T+5 settlement
Stripe payout to ADCB takes T+5 business days in the UAE. Stripe-imposed settlement window, not an InDebted constraint.
ADCB cannot reconcile payments against core banking until the money physically lands. Impacts cycle-end and month-end cut-offs.
■ In progress InDebted continues pushing Stripe, but settlement timing is largely out of our hands. In the interim, both sides to explore solutions — including ADCB confirming internally whether balances can be updated for customers before money lands (i.e. living with T+5 in core banking).

04
Late-stage buckets B5–6
EL, PL and CC accounts in buckets 5 and 6 have very high roll rates — these are functionally recoveries, not active collections.
Current classification prevents meaningful treatment. Can't offer proper payment plans or realistic settlement terms on these accounts.
✗ Open Reclassify as recoveries. Lock interest accrual. Allow InDebted to work them with proper tools — payment plans and settlements (no settlements before 180 DPD+).
05
Settlement authority
Settlement authority is currently capped — even on accounts that are years overdue and where full recovery is unlikely.
Delays and friction on every settlement. Deals are lost while waiting for approval.
InDebted can't deploy capital where it works hardest; offers that would lift overall recovery sit unactioned.
✗ Open Agree a settlement grid that gives InDebted delegated authority where it makes sense. Bank also needs flexibility on aged accounts where the alternative is zero recovery.
Introduce a monthly Settlement Budget per allocation (e.g. AED 250k aggregate). InDebted decides per-account use within the envelope; any overage is netted against the invoice. Maximises portfolio performance with a clear ceiling.
06
KPIs & performance data
No visibility on actual performance data from other agencies.
Cannot assess InDebted on a like-for-like basis.
✗ Open Spin-down reports to benchmark performance. Agree a shared KPI suite — both sides currently measuring different things with different definitions.
07
Account recalls & rotation
ADCB recalls accounts after ~30 days and rotates them to another agency.
30 days prevents Collect from optimising contact time and channel per consumer — the engine has barely learned what works for an account before it's pulled.
Even consumers who have started engaging — opening messages, visiting the portal, agreeing a plan — get recalled before they can make their next payment. Exacerbated by monthly pay cycles common in the UAE: a customer paid mid-month often can't action a balance until the following salary date, which lands outside the 30-day window.
Next agency inherits no context, customer is less likely to re-engage, PTPs in flight are lost.
✗ Open Allow Collect to retain accounts across multiple months by default — and longer where the consumer is showing meaningful engagement (opening messages, visiting the portal, on a payment plan). Lets the engine optimise per consumer and converts engagement into payment.
