Expanded into New Zealand, then Canada. $14M Series A raised through the downturn.
2021
$25M Series B · US market entry
US launch via acquisition. $25M Series B raised. Headcount through 250.

UK market entry. $22.5M raised at double the prior valuation.

A$60M Series C closed. Two acquisitions — including Receive, AI-powered collections software.

Mexico + UAE market entry
LatAm via APLAZO partnership. UAE entity stood up — first live clients, Arabic-language support, regional team built out.
2026
9-market footprint · $1B+ recovered
France and Brazil online. Nine markets, more than a billion dollars returned to lenders worldwide.
