Proposal · Pilot phasing

Two phases. The first earns the second.

We don't ask ADCB to bet the bank on a Day 1 deployment. The pilot starts where the operating model gap is smallest, and expands as the engine proves out.

P1
Months 1 – 3

Express Loans + existing recoveries

All buckets, including early delinquency

Express Loans across all buckets, including early delinquency (1–3) — today InDebted only sees buckets 4–6. Existing recoveries portfolios continue at the current 60% wallet share by geography, with deeper engagement on offshore and hard-to-locate cases as the engine proves out.

  • Express Loans, all buckets including early delinquency (1–3)
  • Recoveries: 60% wallet share by geography, expanding into harder cases
  • Email + SMS + portal channels
  • Pre-approved plan thresholds active
  • Pre-approved settlement matrix active
  • AI Voice + Skip available as additional capability soon
  • Can start tomorrow — no infrastructure work or new hires required
→
P2
Post-pilot

Credit Cards + Personal Loans

Gated on operating-model alignment

Credit Cards and Personal Loans onboarding follows once ADCB has resolved the operating-model items — file formats, settlement authorities, and the rest of the action doc. Until then, taking these portfolios on under current SOPs wouldn't deliver lift; they'd be better served by a traditional agency.

  • Gated on workshop action item completion
  • Credit Cards portfolio fully on platform
  • Personal Loans portfolio fully on platform
  • Quarterly business review cadence
Timeline: Phase 1 can start tomorrow. With the operating-model alignment from Section 05, Phase 2 follows naturally as ADCB's items close out. No new hires, no new infrastructure — capacity is already there.
