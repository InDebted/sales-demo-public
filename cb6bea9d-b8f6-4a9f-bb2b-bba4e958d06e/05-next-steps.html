What InDebted is

One platform for modern recovery — in production for a decade.

InDebted is a regulated collections infrastructure platform — three products that handle early-stage reminders, independent escalation, and the compliance + evidence layer behind both.
In production since 2016. Operating in nine jurisdictions under ASIC, FCA, CFPB, OSFI, CBUAE and others. The platform sends more than 40 million regulated messages a month, and has done so without a material regulatory finding. The infrastructure exists today; what's adapted per client is the configuration, the brand, and the cohort shape.

The three products

One layer for each part of the recovery flow.

Each product stands on its own and can be adopted independently. Together they cover the unpaid-toll journey from early reminder to evidenced escalation.

Receive
Early-stage self-serve recovery · client-branded
Soft digital reminders in the “forgot, will get to it” window — SMS, email, push, web, one-tap pay. White-labelled to the client brand.
Toll-road fitLifts the admin-failure cohort (expired card, missed reminder, changed address) before any notice or fee is needed.
Collect
Independent, digital-first escalation · InDebted-branded
Behaviour-led, multi-channel escalation under the InDebted brand — the deliberate “independent third party” signal after the client's in-brand contact has been exhausted.
Toll-road fitHandles the escalation cohort firmly without putting Linkt's name on the firmer conversation. Sub-thousand sweet spot, plus a softer-touch shape for the long tail.
Comply
Compliance + evidence layer · back-end
Firewall + Safeguard + Rules + Audit + Consent. Wraps the other two products — or your existing outbound — with rule-checking, vulnerability scoring, and a regulator-grade audit timeline.
Toll-road fitMakes “balanced, simpler, more transparent” demonstrable on every customer, every day — and produces the evidence a regulator, ombudsman or journalist would ask for.
The toll-road framing

Why this matters for Transurban specifically.

Three reasons the platform fits the tolling profile — and the operating environment it sits in.

A·B

Two cohorts, not one

Most unpaid-toll balances sit in the sub-thousand sweet spot that digital collections handles best. But unlike a telco, you can't disconnect a car from the road — so a long tail of larger balances builds up that needs a different shape. The platform handles both.

L→I

Two brands, one journey

Linkt stays on the in-brand reminders (Receive). The firmer escalation arrives from a recognisably separate sender (InDebted). That separation keeps Linkt out of the part of the conversation most likely to generate a screenshot.

REG

Evidence as a by-product

NSW Toll Reform — “balanced, simpler, more transparent” — translates into specific obligations. Comply makes each one demonstrable per customer, per day, per channel — without a quarterly forensic project.

Operating principles

How the platform behaves, regardless of which product is in play.

The six things that hold true across Receive, Collect, and Comply — useful as a yardstick for anything we'd propose together.

01
Digital-first economics

Cost-to-resolve has to sit below the balance being recovered. Letters and call cycles don't pencil at toll-road balance sizes.

02
Behaviour-led, not template

Channel, timing, and content variants chosen from engagement history rather than fired at every cohort the same way.

03
Brand-grade by design

Complaint and dispute signals read alongside recovery — so a lift in recovery that comes with a spike in complaints isn't a hidden trade.

04
Evidence as a by-product

Every message, signal, decision, and override sits on a single audit timeline per customer — produced as work happens, not after the fact.

05
Jurisdiction-mapped

160+ production rules across nine jurisdictions, maintained by a dedicated legal team. Clients inherit changes rather than maintain them.

06
AU data, AU resident

Sydney datacentre. SOC 2 Type II + ISO 27001. AU data does not leave AU.
