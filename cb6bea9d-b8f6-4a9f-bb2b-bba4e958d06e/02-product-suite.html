Section 02

The unpaid-toll journey, mapped.
Three layers, one platform.

Receive intercepts early before things escalate. Collect handles the lift on accounts that didn't self-resolve. Comply wraps every interaction with rule-checks, vulnerability signals, and audit-grade evidence — so a regulator, an ombudsman, or a journalist can be answered the same day, not next quarter.

The journey today

From toll capture to resolution — where each layer plugs in.

Today most of the unpaid-toll journey is owned by Linkt and the back-office, with a fall-off to enforcement at the end. Here's where InDebted's three products fit between the two — and why each one matters at a different stage of the journey.

Receive · Layer 1
Catch what self-resolves
A large share of unpaid tolls is admin failure — wrong card on file, forgotten reminder, changed address. Receive lifts those before they need a notice.
Collect · Layer 2
Do the lift, keep the brand
Behaviour-led, digital-first cadences on the accounts that need real engagement — without sounding like a debt collector or generating an ombudsman complaint.
Comply · Layer 3
Defensible, transparent, auditable
Wraps the other two — and your own outbound — with rule-checking, vulnerability flagging, and an evidence trail that maps to NSW Toll Reform, the ACL, and the relevant State Ombudsman Schemes.
The three products

One platform, three products. Adopt one, two, or all three.

The platform is designed to be adopted in layers — you don't need to take all three to start. Most engagements begin with one product against a single cohort, then expand as the evidence builds.

01
Receive
Live AU
Early-stage self-serve recovery
AI-powered software that intercepts customers in the “forgot, will get to it” window. Soft digital nudges, frictionless self-service, no admin fees — designed to lift the easy ones before they need escalation.
  • White-labelled — runs under the Linkt brand
  • SMS, email, app-push, web
  • Behaviour-led send timing
  • One-tap pay / update-card / self-serve plans
Fit for Transurban
A lot of unpaid tolls are admin failure, not refusal. Receive is built for exactly that cohort and stays in your brand.
02
Collect
Independent, digital-first escalation
The escalation engine that takes over when Receive's in-brand contact hasn't resolved. Runs under the InDebted brand — a deliberate signal to the customer that they're now talking to an independent third party — without dragging Linkt's name into the firmer contact.
  • InDebted-branded — the independent-escalation signal
  • Multi-channel (SMS / email / voice / push / portal)
  • Embedded payment plans and self-serve resolution
  • Hardship triage routes off the standard cadence
Fit for Transurban
Lets Linkt stay soft and in-brand at Receive, and have a separate independent escalation step that doesn't carry Linkt's logo into the firmer conversation.
03
Comply
Compliance + evidence layer
The regulated-communications layer that sits under the other two products — and over your own outbound. Every message rule-checked before send, every interaction evidenced, every vulnerability signal flagged in real time.
  • Firewall: pre-send rule-check on every outbound
  • Safeguard: real-time vulnerability identification
  • Rules: 160+ production rules, jurisdiction-mapped
  • Audit + Consent: one-customer-one-timeline evidence
Fit for Transurban
NSW Toll Reform asks for “balanced, simpler, more transparent”. Comply makes that demonstrable on every customer, every day.

How it sits alongside Linkt

The platform doesn't replace anything you have. Your back-office remains the system of record. InDebted plugs in as the recovery layer between “invoice unpaid” and “referral to enforcement” — Receive runs under Linkt's brand for early contact; Collect runs under InDebted's brand when an independent escalation step is the right move.
