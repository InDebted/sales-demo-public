The three layers

Three layers, from arrears to resolved.

Receive moves an account from arrears to resolved across three layers. Decisioning chooses what to do, Engagement resolves the customer, and Governance keeps every step safe and auditable.

Layer 01 · Decisioning

Chooses what to do. AI scores every account and picks the next best action, with your team designing and testing the strategy on top.

  • AI-powered behaviour and risk scores on every account
  • Embedded A/B testing on every strategy
  • Dynamic partner outsourcing — hand off to Collect when it makes sense

Layer 02 · Engagement

Resolves the customer. Omnichannel journeys and self-serve meet each customer where they are, tuned to how they behave.

  • Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Webchat and Letter — AI picks the best channel per account
  • White-label self-serve payment portal in your brand
  • Self-serve has cut inbound payment-arrangement calls by up to 70%

Layer 03 · Governance

Keeps it safe and auditable. The audit trail is a by-product of the work — not a report to assemble after the fact.

  • Every decision logged and explainable — no black boxes
  • Configurable rules: exclusion logic, affordability checks, contact limits, time windows
  • Mandatory disclosures embedded directly in templates
Strategy builder + AI

Strategy design and AI, in your hands.

Receive combines strategy design and payment orchestration into one platform. AI runs underneath — but with explicit guardrails so your team stays in control.

How we think about AI in Receive

  • Trained on your data only. Your models are never mixed with other sources.
  • All decisions are tracked. No black boxes — every AI decision is traceable and auditable.
  • Operates within your boundaries. Receive's AI stays in line with your rules — regional restrictions, contact limits, compliance constraints.
  • Features can be turned on or off. You decide how AI is used. Every feature is controlled by you, no surprises.
Three model scores

Payments score — probability the account repays. Self-serve score — probability it resolves with no agent. Collections score — overall account health. Together they direct how and where each account is worked.

Send-time optimisation

The model learns when each customer opens and acts on email, then times the message to match — lifting engagement without adding contact volume.

Dynamic outsourcing

When an account is better served by Collect (third-party), Receive routes it natively. One direct integration manages allocations, accounts and performance in one place.
