A

Scoping

Defining what we're collecting, on whose behalf, under what rules

~ 4 weeks
What we deliver
  • Portfolio definition document — segmentation, balance bands, DPD ranges
  • Data dictionary — every field, source, validation rule
  • Compliance ruleset — UAE Central Bank (CBUAE), your internal policy
  • Channel-mix proposal — what's allowed, when, with what consent
  • KPI scorecard — agreed definitions before placement starts
Our ask of our client
  • Portfolio owner per segment (single accountable name)
  • Compliance review of our ruleset, with sign-off
  • Sample placement file, real anonymised data
  • Customer consent posture — what's already collected, what's not
  • Approval thresholds — who can settle below what %, plan length, etc.
B

Loading

Building the connection between your systems and Collect

~ 4 weeks
What we deliver
  • SFTP / API integration — daily placements, transactions, direct payments
  • Test placement environment — full lifecycle dry runs
  • Customer Portal — InDebted-branded, EN/AR, mobile-first self-serve
  • Comply firewall configuration — UAE ruleset live, audit trail wired
  • Stripe + payments setup — local processing, settlement configuration
Our ask of our client
  • Test SFTP access, or API access for custom integrations
  • Brand assets — logo, colour palette, tone of voice guide
  • Stripe account portal — KYC business and personnel data confirmed
  • UAT participants — minimum 2 of your ops team + 1 compliance
  • Sign-off on "go live" criteria — what does success look like
C

Operations

The system live, accounts decisioned, customers engaged

Week 8 onwards
What we deliver
  • Daily placements — Collect ingests, validates, decisions
  • Multi-channel engagement — email, SMS, letter, portal
  • Self-serve resolution — settle, plan, dispute in-app
  • Daily reconciliation — payment events flowing back to your systems
  • Specialist escalation queue — for complex intent or vulnerable customers
Our ask of our client
  • Disputes queue — single inbox or system to receive escalations
  • Operational owner — single point of contact for live issues
  • Defined hardship policy — terms we're allowed to offer
  • Off-portfolio actions — timely transaction notifications
Z

Reporting

Every metric, every event, queryable and shareable

Continuous
What we deliver
  • Live client portal — KPIs refreshed continuously
  • Daily KPI digest — placements, contacts, payments, settlements
  • Weekly operations review — anomalies, trends, action items
  • Monthly business review — spin-down, roll-rate, cohort performance
  • Regulator-ready audit pack — exportable in <1 hour for any account
Our ask of our client
  • Confirmation of agreed KPI definitions before pilot launch
  • Regular review cadence — weekly/monthly scheduled, not ad-hoc
  • Internal stakeholders for the monthly business review (ops + risk + finance)
  • Feedback channel for KPI definitions to evolve
