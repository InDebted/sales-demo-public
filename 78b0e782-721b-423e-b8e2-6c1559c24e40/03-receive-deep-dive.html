Key benefits

Control. Flexibility. Intelligence.

Three pillars that shape what Receive does for an internal team. Each is independently valuable; together they're what makes the platform an actual operating system rather than a tool.

Control

Manage end-to-end with compliance-ready architecture and powerful integrations.

  • Set controls for any regulatory environment
  • Connect to your existing tech stack (Salesforce, FICO, Twilio, Stripe, more)
  • Maintain a single source of truth

Flexibility

Design, test, and launch collections strategies with ease.

  • Build omnichannel journeys with no-code drag-and-drop
  • Create branded, self-serve payment portals
  • Personalise collections without manual overhead

Intelligence

Make every decision smarter with AI-driven insights and real-time performance data.

  • AI-powered behaviour and risk scores
  • Embedded A/B testing on every strategy
  • Dynamic partner outsourcing (hand-off to Collect when it makes sense)
Strategy builder + AI

Strategy design and AI, in your hands.

Receive combines strategy design and payment orchestration into one platform. AI runs underneath — but with explicit guardrails so your team stays in control.

How we think about AI in Receive

  • Trained on your data only. Your models are never mixed with other sources.
  • All decisions are tracked. No black boxes — every AI decision is traceable and auditable.
  • Operates within your boundaries. Receive's AI stays in line with your rules — regional restrictions, contact limits, compliance constraints.
  • Features can be turned on or off. You decide how AI is used. Every feature is controlled by you, no surprises.
Behavioural scoring

Models score every account on probability of repayment and probability to self-serve — directing how and where each account performs best.

Dynamic outsourcing

When an account is better served by Collect (third-party), Receive routes it natively. One direct integration manages allocations, accounts and performance in one place.

Architecture

Receive overlays your tech stack.

Not a rip-and-replace. Receive sits on top of your system of record and your channel/payment providers, unifying tools, teams, and data flows.

Source

System of record

Your existing core platform — Salesforce, C&R, custom systems. Receive ingests via API or batch.
