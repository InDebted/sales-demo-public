Section 04

A phased pilot that starts at zero risk.

A quick recap of what we've walked through, then a concrete proposal for what we'd do next — designed so IAG carries no integration risk, no live-traffic risk, and no commitment until the evidence is in front of you.

What we propose

Three phases. The first one carries no risk.

A path that lets IAG see Comply on your own data before any live integration. Each phase is gated on the evidence from the previous one — you decide whether to proceed.

PHASE 01 · ~2 WEEKS

Retrospective Audit

A sample of historical outbound & inbound communications — run through Comply offline. You receive a report: what Safeguard would have flagged, what Firewall would have blocked, mapped to RG 271, NCCP, and GICOP.

Risk to IAG: none. No integration, no live traffic, no commitment.

PHASE 02 · ~4 WEEKS

Shadow Mode

A defined portfolio segment routed through Comply in observe-only mode. Decisions are produced and logged; nothing is blocked or escalated. Your team validates the calls against their own judgement.

Risk to IAG: low. Live data flows, no action taken.

PHASE 03 · ~6 WEEKS

Enforce & Expand

Switch on enforcement on the agreed segment — Firewall blocks non-compliant sends, Safeguard escalates vulnerability signals to your specialist teams. Expand by line of business once the operating rhythm is proven.

Risk to IAG: controlled. Scope you set, segment you choose.

To move forward

What we'd ask of IAG to start Phase 01.

The retrospective audit needs three things. None of them require a live integration.
